What to take away

✓
Monitoring evolved because architectures demanded it. Reactive tools built for monoliths simply don't scale to hundreds of ephemeral microservices generating millions of events per second.
✓
Logs, metrics, and traces are the foundation — but profiles, RUM, and synthetic signals complete the picture for modern user-facing systems.
✓
Standardisation pays compound dividends. OTel and ECS reduce instrumentation cost today and unlock AI-assisted analysis tomorrow.
✓
The industry is converging on unified platforms. Point-solution sprawl is the new technical debt — the winners will consolidate on open, AI-native stores.
