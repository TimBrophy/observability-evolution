04 — Standards & Unification

The fragmentation tax
is costing you time

Before standards, every team instrumented differently. Correlating a database metric with an application trace with a Kubernetes log meant custom ETL, brittle mappings, and slow queries across siloed stores.

⚡
Faster correlationShared field names mean joins work out of the box — no translation layer between signals.
🔓
Vendor portabilityInstrument once with OTel — point the exporter at any backend. No lock-in.
🤖
AI-ready dataConsistent schema is the foundation that makes LLM-assisted root-cause analysis actually work.
OpenTelemetry

CNCF standard for collecting logs, metrics, and traces. SDKs for every major language. Adopted by every major vendor. The de-facto collection layer.

Elastic Common Schema (ECS)

Field-level naming convention mapping any data source to a unified schema. Powers cross-source search and ML without manual mapping.
