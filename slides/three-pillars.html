03 — Core Data Signals

The three pillars of observability

📋

Logs

Immutable, timestamped records of discrete events. The most human-readable signal — and the highest volume.

2024-01-15T14:23:01Z ERROR
checkout timeout after 5002ms
📈

Metrics

Numeric measurements sampled over time. Efficient, queryable, perfect for alerting and trending at massive scale.

http_requests_total{
status="500"} 47
🔗

Traces

End-to-end records of a single request's journey across services. The key to understanding distributed latency.

trace_id: 4f2a9c8e…
span: checkout → payment
Logs tell you what happened. Metrics tell you how often. Traces tell you why it was slow. You need all three to ask — and answer — arbitrary questions about your system's behaviour.
