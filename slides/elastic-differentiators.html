06 — Elastic's Differentiators

Why organisations choose Elastic

🔍
One store for everything

Logs, metrics, traces, security events, and business data in a single Elasticsearch index — no cross-tool pivoting mid-investigation.

⚡
Search-first architecture

Ad-hoc queries across petabytes in seconds. Most observability tools are purpose-built BI tools dressed as monitoring — Elastic is a search engine first.

🤖
AI built on your data

Elastic AI Assistant uses the LLM of your choice over your own indexed data — no vendor lock-in, no data leaving your environment.

🔓
Open by default

OTel-native ingestion, ECS schema, open source core. Instrument once, retain the ability to switch backends — Elastic competes on value, not lock-in.

The Elastic advantage: observability, security, and search on one platform means a single team, one licence to optimise, and AI that can reason across all your data simultaneously — not just the silo it was given.
