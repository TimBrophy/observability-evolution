01 — History & Evolution

How we got here

1980s–90s
Network Monitoring Begins Reactive

SNMP, ping checks, and MRTG graphs. Engineers watched dashboards for red lights. Systems were simple enough that "up or down" was a meaningful question.

2000s
APM & Log Aggregation Reactive

Nagios, Zabbix, New Relic APM arrive. Log files centralised. Still fundamentally reactive — you knew about a problem after users told you.

2010s
The Cloud & Microservices Explosion Reactive → Struggling

Containers, Kubernetes, serverless. Services multiplied 100×. Existing tools couldn't correlate across hundreds of ephemeral components. The monitoring model broke.

2020s
Observability & AI-Driven Operations Proactive

Distributed tracing, unified telemetry, machine learning on streams. The question shifts from "is it down?" to "why is it slow, for whom, and what will break next?"
